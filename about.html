About Gogaku

The Japanese language is a journey

When studying Japanese, multiple resources are often used in combination — a textbook or website, a dictionary, flashcards. When it's time to practice, it's not uncommon to come across grammar that you haven't learned yet, or see kanji you haven't seen before.

Gogaku walks the journey with you

Gogaku (語学 – ごがく, "language study") is a language learning platform created to integrate multiple resources into a cohesive learning experience to meet your study needs, whether you're a beginner or an advanced learner. If you need to start out from the beginning, lessons and flashcards are available to help you build a strong foundation. Need to look up a word or kanji? The dictionary is always available. Want to practice reading and writing? Chat provides a conversation partner that matches your comprehension level. As you progress, Gogaku adapts to your journey.
