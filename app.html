The app

Gogaku contains structured lessons, a dynamic dictionary, spaced-repetition flashcards, and a conversation partner that only speaks the Japanese you have actually learned. Each tool feeds the next, so what you learn on Monday is what you are practicing on Friday.

How it works

Vocabulary flows into Flashcards, grammar flows into Chat, and everything you study shapes the conversations you practice with.
