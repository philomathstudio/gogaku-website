How Gogaku Works

Four tools, one app.

Gogaku uses four integrated tools to enhance your learning experience.

Lessons

In lessons, you will learn fundamental Japanese grammar topics with examples, see dialogue in action, and acquire new vocabulary and kanji. Once a lesson is complete, the next one becomes available, and occasionally other features will be unlocked, as well. Vocabulary and kanji from completed lessons unlocks a flashcard deck for it, and grammar topics become available for practice in chat.

Dictionary

In the dictionary, you have a comprehensive overview of all vocabulary, kanji, kana, and radicals in Gogaku. Each type contains different kinds of information. For instance, adjectives and verbs in vocabulary entries provide tables of their conjugations, while kanji entries include common compounds for you to see how words relate. You can search for a word or filter through the dictionary by type, part of speech, category, and more. You can also add to — or create — flashcard decks for studying.

Flashcards

In flashcards, you can study lesson decks or create your own deck. When studying a deck, swipe right if you know the word, up if you're unsure, and left if you don't know it. Flashcards uses a spaced repetition system (SRS), an algorithm to show you cards you struggle with more often, and brings them back right as you are about to forget them to reinforce your recallability. Words you need more practice with will also appear more often in Chat.

Chat

Chat uses two distinct modes: Tomodachi and Sensei. Each has independent uses and memory so you can utilize each for different purposes. If you need additional examples or a more detailed explanation, Sensei Mode will speak to you in English and provide examples in Japanese, similarly to how lessons example sentences works. You can ask things like, "what is this?" "how do you say this?" or "what's the difference between this and that?"

Tomodachi Mode is a Japanese practice friend who only speaks to you in Japanese, but it is designed to match your Japanese grammar and vocabulary comprehension level. Every word it uses is one you've been exposed to. It will introduce new words naturally into conversation, however, and a separate flashcard deck is created composed of words Tomodachi introduces separately from Lessons. Tomodachi also has a unique personality, likes and dislikes, an occupation and goals. As your relationship with it improves, it may share more about itself.
