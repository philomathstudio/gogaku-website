Learn, search, study, and practice Japanese

Gogaku is a Japanese learning app for iOS, built around structured lessons, a dynamic dictionary, spaced-repetition flashcards, and a conversation partner that only speaks the Japanese you have actually learned. Alongside it, this site hosts free references anyone can use without an account.

The app

Four tools that feed into each other. Finish a lesson and its vocabulary becomes a flashcard deck while its grammar unlocks in chat. Tomodachi Mode speaks only in words you already know, introducing new ones one at a time. Coming to the App Store soon. See what the app does.

Dictionary

The app's full dictionary, searchable in romaji, kana, kanji, or English. Verbs and adjectives include conjugation tables, kanji entries include radical breakdowns and stroke order, and example sentences carry furigana. Search the dictionary.

Kana

The complete hiragana and katakana syllabaries with readings and stroke order — the first thing to learn, and the reference you will come back to for months. Open the charts.

Articles

Longer writing on the things that trip learners up: particles, verb forms, kanji readings, and honest notes on what actually works when you sit down to study. Start reading.
