What's Inside

Four tools, one app.

Most learners end up juggling a textbook app, a dictionary, a flashcard app, and something for speaking practice — none of which know anything about each other. Gogaku puts all four under one roof and gives them a shared memory of what you've studied.

課

Structured Lessons

Twenty-five lessons from absolute beginner upward. Grammar, vocabulary, and dialogue, built progressively so each lesson stands on the one before it.

辞

Real Dictionary

Nearly 10,000 entries with full conjugation tables, JLPT levels, example sentences, and kanji breakdowns by radical. Search anything, instantly.

札

SRS Flashcards

Spaced repetition that follows the SM-2 schedule. Build decks from lesson vocabulary or anything you look up, and review on the interval your memory actually needs.

話

Conversation Practice

Tomodachi holds a real conversation with you in Japanese — and stays strictly inside the words and grammar you've covered. Long-press any word for a translation. Furigana on demand.

The Difference

It won't talk over your head.

Drop most language chatbots into a beginner conversation and they answer in fluent, natural Japanese you have no way to parse. Tomodachi works from your actual vocabulary list — the words from lessons you've finished and decks you've built — and won't reach past it. When it does introduce something new, it tells you, and the word goes into your deck rather than sailing by. That constraint is the whole point, and it's the part that took the longest to build.
