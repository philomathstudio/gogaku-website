Explore Japanese with Gogaku

Gogaku (語学 – ごがく, "the study of foreign languages, or linguistics") is a Japanese language learning platform designed for self-determined individuals. This site is an open-reference resource, providing a dictionary and a growing library of grammar articles.

The Gogaku app will take you from reference to practice, building retention through structured lessons, an integrated dictionary, intuitive flashcards, and a dynamic Japanese conversation assistant with two distinct practice modes.

The app

The Gogaku app is the right place for beginners seeking to start their Japanese journey and for seasoned learners alike. See what the app does.

Japanese dictionary

Need to find a word in Japanese? The dictionary contains all kinds of useful information, from conjugation tables for verbs and adjectives, to common compounds for kanji. Search the dictionary.
